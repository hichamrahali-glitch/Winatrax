    How much does each country trade per inhabitant?

    Per inhabitant = a reporter's imports (or exports) of goods with the whole world in a year, divided by its population that year. Countries are ranked by imports per inhabitant; Luxembourg is pinned to the top row, and the number before each other country is its rank.     Methodology

    How the figures are made

    Data sources

    • Trade: Eurostat, EU trade since 1988 by HS2-4-6 and CN8 (Comext, DS-045409). Luxembourg as reporter, all partner countries, monthly and annual, 1999 to mid-2026.
    • Trade of other countries: the same dataset, each reporter's trade with the whole world, all goods, annual.
    • Population: World Bank, World Development Indicators, population total (SP.POP.TOTL), 1999–2025.

    Measures

    • Value in euros at current prices, not adjusted for inflation.
    • Weight in tonnes (Eurostat reports units of 100 kg).
    • Goods only. Trade in services, such as finance, is not included.

    Trading partners (world map)

    • Stepping by year shows calendar-year totals (1999–2025). Stepping by month shows, for each month, the total of the 12 months up to and including it (to June 2026), so seasonal swings do not move the ranking.
    • Partners are ranked by that total; the five largest are highlighted and ranks 6–25 open with "show more".
    • The trend chart for a selected country uses calendar-year totals.
    • For trade inside the EU the partner is the member state the goods were shipped from or to, which can be a transit country such as Belgium or the Netherlands. For imports from outside the EU it is the country of origin.
    • Partners Eurostat keeps confidential are counted in totals and rankings but cannot be placed on the map.

    Products (world map, click a country)

    • Products follow the customs nomenclature, which gets more specific with each pair of digits: category (2 digits, HS chapter), product group (4), product type (6) and specific product (8, EU CN code).
    • Categories for each country are given per calendar year from 1999 to 2025.
    • The deeper levels are prepared for the 40 largest partners, for 2025. Very small items are grouped as "not itemised".
    • The partner chart under the map shows each country's share of Luxembourg's imports (or exports) of the product you open. For a category it uses the same period as the map; for product groups, product types and specific products it loads the calendar year shown on the map from the Eurostat API, covering all partners. The seven largest partners are shown separately and the rest as "other partners".

    International comparison

    • Imports per inhabitant = a country's imports of goods from the whole world in a year ÷ its population that year. Exports per inhabitant are computed the same way.
    • Countries are the 27 EU member states and the United Kingdom, which reports until 2019.
    • The EU27 reference (dashed lines) is the sum of the 27 members' trade divided by the sum of their populations.
    • Countries are ranked by imports per inhabitant; Luxembourg is pinned to the top row.

    Limits

    • A country's trade includes goods passing through on their way elsewhere, which inflates the figures for logistics hubs such as Belgium, the Netherlands and Luxembourg.
    • Totals for imports and exports within the EU are reported separately by each country and do not always match the partner's figures.
    • The latest months are provisional and may be revised by Eurostat.
