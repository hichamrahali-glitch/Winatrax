    How much does each country trade per inhabitant?

    Per inhabitant = a reporter's imports (or exports) of goods with the whole world in a year, divided by its population that year. Countries are ranked by imports per inhabitant; Luxembourg is pinned to the top row, and the number before each other country is its rank. Dashed lines mark the EU27 level (the sum of the 27 members' trade divided by their population). The United Kingdom reports until 2019. Goods only, not services. Sources: Eurostat Dataset DS-045409 (EU trade since 1988), World Bank World Development Indicators (SP.POP.TOTL).

    Methodology

    How the figures are made

    Every number on this page comes from two public datasets, prepared in four steps that anyone can follow and run again.

    1. Download

      Eurostat Dataset provides the trade in goods and the World Bank provides the population figures. Both are free to download, and the exact queries we used are written down, so the same files can be fetched again.

    2. Prepare

      Years, products and trade flows are matched up, and quantities are converted to tonnes. Where a figure is missing in the source it stays missing here, and partial years are kept out of the annual comparisons.

    3. Calculate

      Partners are ranked by the value or the weight of their trade, and trade per inhabitant is simply trade divided by population. The same rule is applied to every country and every year.

    4. Publish

      The prepared figures are bundled into the small data files this page reads, so the map and the comparison open straight from the folder. Running node scripts/build-data.mjs rebuilds them from the same input.

    • SimpleTwo sources and a short set of rules, all set out on this page.
    • RobustNothing is filled in, smoothed or guessed, so a gap in the source stays visible as a gap.
    • OpenThe sources, the prepared data and the build script all travel with the site.
    • Easy to useNo install and no server. Open the page and start exploring.

    Goods only; values are not adjusted for inflation. Trade hubs, reporting differences and revisions are important limitations. Data retrieved on 14 and 15 October 2026.
